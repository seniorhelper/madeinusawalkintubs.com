Walk-in tub manufacturers that say they build in the USA

Published by Aging Safely Baths. Facts checked October 15, 2026; questions added October 22, 2026.

Direct answer: As of October 15, 2026, eight walk-in tub makers publish U.S. production statements on their own websites: American Tubs (Southern California), Bestbath (Caldwell, Idaho and Sparta, Tennessee), Boca Walk-In Tubs (Woodstock, Georgia), Ella's Bubbles (Chicago), Hydro Dimensions (Temecula, California), Meditub, Safe Step (Tennessee) and Universal Tubs. Their wording ranges from full "made in" claims to assembly claims.

We built this list by reading each maker's own site, not reviews or ads. For each brand you get the exact words it uses, the place it names, what kind of claim that wording is under the FTC's Made in USA rules, and the warranty terms it publishes. Brands are in alphabetical order.

How to use this list

A maker's website is a starting point, not proof. The FTC expects a company making a U.S. origin claim to have evidence behind it, and to update the claim if its sourcing changes. Before you buy, ask the dealer to confirm the claim for the exact model you are quoted, in writing. Our walk-in tub dealer question list has the wording to use.

Pay attention to the type of claim, too. "Assembled in Chicago" and "made in Caldwell, Idaho" are both real, specific statements, but they promise different things. Neither one is a knock on the brand. Assembly in the U.S. means American jobs and a factory you can contact. It just is not the same claim as all or virtually all U.S. content.

Brands not on this list

Several well-known walk-in tub brands are missing because we could not find a clear statement on their own site about where their tubs are made, or could not load the relevant pages when we checked. That does not mean their tubs are imported. It means you should ask. If a maker publishes a statement we missed, we will add it with the source and date.

Common questions

How many walk-in tub makers say they build in the United States?

As of October 15, 2026, eight walk-in tub makers published U.S. production statements on their own websites. The table on this page quotes each one's exact wording and the location it names, with a dated link to the source.

Is a maker's "Made in USA" statement certified by the government?

No. The FTC does not pre-approve or certify origin claims. Each statement on this page is the maker's own claim, and the maker is responsible for having proof behind it.
